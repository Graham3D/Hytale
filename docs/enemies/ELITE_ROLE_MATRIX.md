Certification describes a role's native binding. At each natural birth, the existing world-spawn classifier must still prove a hostile actor, authored combat level, ordinary defeat lifecycle, and no protected/QA/campaign ownership. `Enabled` is the independent rollout gate; a role without a native world-spawn entry has no natural birth to promote. Super Uniques require an authored template; they are never random. Elemental resistance assumes at least one nonimmune channel and is rechecked against the frozen combat profile at birth.
