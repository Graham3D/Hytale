# Combat damage and NPC profile trace

This is an operator diagnostic. Use `/rpg-trace combat start`, reproduce one incoming and one outgoing hit, then use `/rpg-trace combat last 2` and `/rpg-trace combat stop`. Aim at an NPC and run `/rpg-trace combat inspect` to compare its RPG profile with its live native Health. No test server is started by this workflow.

The active JSONL file is `mods/InigmasGames_HytaleRPGPhase00Audit/logs/rpg/combat-trace.jsonl` in the active RPG save. The existing bounded trace writer rotates and retains eight compressed segments. Each session lasts at most 60 seconds and accepts at most 2,048 hit and NPC profile events. At most 16 sessions can be active. Each queued record reserves 32 KiB in an 8 MiB queue; oversized records and full queues are counted as dropped, and a later `TRACE_GAP` marks incomplete evidence. Serialization and disk writes run on the writer thread. `status` reports completion and reconciliation counters. Disconnect, world unload, and plugin shutdown end capture.

## Per-hit fields

`sessionId` selects the operator's capture. `hitId` is unique to one session and damage packet; `sequence` orders emitted events; `rootId`, `strikeId`, and `contactId` retain existing Hywind IDs where available. Native packets without an authored ID receive stable IDs for their captured packet. `sourceId`, `ownerId`, and `targetId` distinguish the physical source, Hywind credit owner, and victim. `direction` is relative to the selected player. A packet shared by two active players produces a separate personal record for each and shares its root and contact IDs.

`attempted` is the float amount at the Hywind Gather observer. `accepted` is the amount after native filtering and before RPG absorption. `postAbsorption` is the final native amount at ApplyDamage, or zero when cancelled. `prevented` and `absorbed` are nonnegative boundary differences. `healthBefore` and `healthAfter` are sampled immediately around native ApplyDamage for that same packet. `healthLost` is the observed decrease, capped at prior Health; `overkill` is the final amount beyond prior Health. All damage and Health quantities use native Health points, with native float narrowing retained in stage outputs. Unknown numeric values are JSON `null` with an `UNOBSERVABLE` explanation.

`stages` are ordered numeric input/output operations with owner and typed details. Hywind calculated skill hits expose base power, the selected scaling attribute and its exact effective value, the actual Increased/Reduced/More/Less lists passed to the calculation, skill coefficient, modifier factor, critical outcome, float conversion, committed STR/DEX/INT/WIS/other attribute snapshot, gear revision and affix IDs. The scaling attribute is chosen by the authored skill profile: HEAVY uses STR, LIGHT uses DEX, MAGIC uses INT, and WEAPON_CLASS uses its bound attribute. The other committed attributes remain visible even when they do not contribute to that hit. Managed gear channel packets expose submitted sampled source power, coefficient, applied critical chance where available, equipped item/intrinsic stats, individual affix IDs and values, Physical/Wind/Water/Fire/Earth/Lightning/Void amounts, and penetration. Native enemy hits expose the actual difficulty factor operation, armor/resistance before and after, recipient gear reductions where owned by Hywind, and barrier absorption with identity and remaining capacity when available. `source` and `target` contain the frozen RPG difficulty/level/profile data available at the hit. A stored `difficultyDamageFactor` can include both the 1.8 Hell scalar and an authored level damage ratio; the progression ratio is recorded separately when present.

`reconciliation` is `MATCH`, `MISMATCH`, or `UNOBSERVABLE`. `MISMATCH` means observed per-packet Health loss differs by more than 0.02 Health from `min(HealthBefore, postAbsorption)`; it is not silently adjusted. A native hit can also be blocked, cancelled, or fully absorbed and still have a record. The trace never applies or resubmits damage.

Some native internals have no accessible per-modifier callback. The random native base sample and critical RNG sample, plus any individual native Filter contributions between observed boundaries, are listed as `UNOBSERVABLE`. Actual authored `ModifierBuckets` preserve values and operation order but lack individual source IDs for every passive/status contribution. A record with such a marker supports reconstruction only to the named observed boundary, not a fabricated full equation. Native Health may also change inside ApplyDamage through a mechanism outside the exposed packet amount; this is reported as a reconciliation mismatch.

## NPC profile events and Goblin evidence

`NPC_PROFILE_ADDED` records every NPC added during a same-world capture, including runtime/canonical role, native add reason, environment, spawn configuration, authored-role lookup, exclusion, and whether the world-spawn evidence gate accepts it. `NPC_PROFILE_APPLIED` and `NPC_PROFILE_RESTORED` record the profile, level, difficulty, native baseline, expected and actual maximum Health, current Health, damage factor, and rank after successful native projection. `NPC_PROFILE_NOT_APPLIED` records an explicit reason when `ready` rejects an entity or lacks an authored combat profile; `MISSING_NATIVE_WORLD_SPAWN_EVIDENCE` singles out spawned roles whose native metadata fails the natural classification gate. `NPC_PROFILE_DUPLICATE_OR_STALE` records a rejected ticket. These outcomes are observational and do not enroll excluded NPCs.

The installed Hytale 0.7.0-pre.5.1 assets show that `Goblin_Initial_Breach_Stage2` pastes a site whose `Goblin_Initial_Breach` EncounterManager action spawns 1–2 `Goblin_Scrapper`. Native `ActionSpawn` calls `NPCPlugin.spawnEntity`; `NPCPlugin` adds the NPC with `AddReason.SPAWN` before its post-spawn callback copies the parent's spawn configuration. Hywind's ordinary `classifyNatural` requires world-spawn environment and spawn configuration to be present *at entity add*. This makes missing ordinary profile enrollment for that route a source-supported possibility. The recurring `Goblin_Breach_Stage3` instead pastes a portal and trigger volumes; it contains no direct NPC Spawn action. A connected capture is required to identify which event the player observed, its actual NPC roles, their profile outcomes, and the hit that killed a Goblin. Absence of Goblin rows in the earlier limited R256 trace is not proof of exclusion.

To investigate a weak event Goblin, start capture before approaching the event, inspect the NPC while alive, hit it once, then use `last 1` and stop. Compare `NPC_PROFILE_ADDED` with `NPC_PROFILE_APPLIED` or `NPC_PROFILE_NOT_APPLIED` for its entity ID, and compare the hit's `maximumHealthAtGather`, `healthBefore`, `postAbsorption`, `healthLost`, and `overkill`. The connected capture decides whether a missing profile, native baseline, unusually large player hit, or another mechanic explains the kill.
