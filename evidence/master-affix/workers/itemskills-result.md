## Exact cross-owner wiring

1. In `HyArpgPlugin`, register `new NativeItemSkillAvailabilitySystem(loadouts)` after `gearEquipment.new Tick()` and before `skillExecutionSystem`. On `PlayerDisconnectEvent`, world transfer, and any actor removal that stops equipment publication, call `loadouts.detachItemSkillAvailability(playerUuid)`; on reconnect, the registered tick republishes the current valid snapshot. Keep `GearAffixRuntime.ENABLED` until main verifies capability; WA-144 currently remains gated.
2. In the main-owned native `HytaleDamageLifecycleSystems.Application` observer composition, construct `ItemSkillTriggerRuntime.DirectHit(world,actor,item,target,root,contact,acceptedSnapshot,direct,hostile,noProc,reflected,blocked,acceptedDirectDamage,procCoefficient,protectedTarget)` from an authoritative completed *item* hit. Call `ItemSkillTriggerRuntime.applied(hit,now,itemSkillQueue)` once after native Apply; do not call from Filter. `AppliedReceipt` presently exposes actual HP loss but not accepted post-filter direct damage, so the damage owner must add that exact correlated field; positive absorbed hits may have zero HP loss and remain eligible. Register `new NativeItemSkillBlockObserver(itemSkillTriggers,itemSkillQueue)` with a queue that only dispatches at a later valid world step.
3. Add an item child entry to `HytaleSkillExecutionSystem`/`SkillExecutionService` (resources2 owner). Inputs: `ItemSkillTriggerRuntime.Child` plus current world actor/target refs; preserve `child.source()` and exact `item()`, fixed `rank()==1`, `noProc`, `noCrit`, `noLeech`, no linked passives, `additionalMana()==0`, and root identity. Check target, LOS, protection and legal source power, then invoke the existing Fire Bolt, Frost Bolt or Minor Heal executor. The ordinary slot request path cannot do this: `SkillExecutionService.validate` reads an assigned slot and compiled plan, while item procs require no slot and cannot teach the skill.
4. In `HytaleSkillExecutionSystem.Port.itemGrantedSkillLevels`, pass learned ownership into `GearSkillRanks` or return 0 for learned-only WA-121–133 when executing an unlearned WA-144 grant. The current port applies `GearSkillRanks.bonus(snapshot,profile)` without that distinction.
5. In `HytaleSupportSystem` and `SupportRuntime` (cross-owner), introduce a distinct item Aura key `actor/itemUuid/skillId`, fixed rank-1 canonical context, source-item validity callback, separate item allocation/field token and cleanup, and manual/item same-skill coexistence. `HytaleSupportSystem.Port.valid` currently requires the live loadout slot's plan hash, and `SupportRuntime.execute` keys by skill and toggles a same-skill Aura off. Those current contracts cannot safely activate WA-148–150 from an item without edits to those owners. Sentinel needs an explicit item-bound context/port whose sustain is free only for D08 while preserving recipient and cleanup rules. Main calls this sync on validated equipment publication and clears it on removal/death/transfer.
6. Register each ID in the main-owned capability gate only after its above consumer and tests pass; do not count queued children or definition-only Aura entries as functional.
