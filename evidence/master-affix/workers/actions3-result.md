The item `Primary` route points at each immutable profile root *before* native chain acceptance. `HytaleGearEquipment.Use` rejects a stale root; the main-owned `NativeGearAttackAcceptance.Prechain` registered in `HyArpgPlugin` then captures the valid snapshot/root, and the managed damage codecs receive the variant leaf's `RpgProcSelector` and `RpgProcCoefficient`. The main-owned plugin already registers `NativeTwinAssaultGate`; the combat owner has already connected child NoProc/NoLeech handling. Do not duplicate those registrations. The twin second selector and offhand calculator predate this continuation and are preserved.

Required cross-owner work before promotion/enablement:

2. **Main/carrier owner:** after codec and actual native stack roundtrip gates, promote the 90 staged staff/wand/book/shield/bomb bindings with `Complete-NativeGearCarriers.ps1 -Promote`. Keep the instructed `ENABLED` gate closed until consumers pass. The authored shield is excluded from WA-008/014 by H eligibility; focus C-star WA-008 requires an explicit legal-catalog change after the native weapon-attack path is accepted. `GearDropGenerator.eligible` currently admits only WA-017–022 C-star exceptions, so focus WA-008 does not naturally roll.
