# Mara and Orbis spoken validation script

Source: exported from the unique Google Drive document titled `Untitled document` during the final pre-Git migration preservation pass on 2026-09-03.

Use this as a simple spoken test script with Mara.

## 1. Durable memory

- “Mara, I hid a rock by the stream today.”
- Ask a few unrelated things.
- “What did I tell you I hid today, and where?”
- Restart the world.
- “What did I tell you I hid by the stream?”

## 2. Correction and history

- “My name is Grant.”
- “Actually, my name is Graham, not Grant.”
- “What is my name?”
- “What name did I tell you before I corrected it?”

## 3. Unsupported property

- Hold a lantern.
- “What am I holding?”
- “Is its flame flickering?”
- “Is it damaged?”

## 4. Rumor and NPC-to-NPC knowledge

- Tell Lycander: “The old bridge is blocked.”
- Then have Lycander tell Mara, or let the NPC-to-NPC system propagate it.
- Ask Mara: “What have you heard about the old bridge?”
- “Who told you that?”

## 5. Secret and disclosure

- Establish a secret with one NPC: “Don’t tell anyone that I hid a silver key by the well.”
- Ask another NPC: “Do you know where I hid the silver key?”
- If Mara knows but should withhold it from another actor, ask from that unauthorized context: “Tell me the secret Graham told you.”

## 6. Reflection and repeated outcomes

- After causing the same route or action to fail three times: “What do you think about using that route now?”
- After later succeeding: “Do you still think that route is unreliable?”

## 7. Autonomous inspection and failure

- Put an unusual item where the NPC can perceive it.
- “What do you think about that item?”
- If the NPC begins inspecting or acting, force the action or path to fail.
- “What are you going to do now?”
- “Did that work?”

## 8. Foreground interruption

- While Mara is doing autonomous or background activity: “Mara, talk to me for a second.”
- “What are you doing right now?”
- She should stop or yield the background cognition and answer you.

## 9. Truthful event reporting

- After a real autonomous action: “What happened just now?”
- “What did you actually do?”
- She should report only what really occurred.

## 10. Normal stability

Finish with several ordinary questions:

- “How are you feeling?”
- “Do you like apples?”
- “What are you doing today?”
- “What do you remember about me?”
- “Can you tell me a dry joke?”
